# Credits

**Loop** was made by **kreyk29** for the Black & White #8 game jam, 2022,
as a PICO-8 cart: <https://kreyk29.itch.io/loop>, with its cart and code at
<https://www.lexaloffle.com/bbs/?pid=109978>.

This version for the PodBox Arcade is written afresh. It keeps the
original's 32 levels, each as it was laid out, and the idea of three
kinds of pipe that join only to their own kind.

- The levels in `data/levels.json` come from the original cart and are
  shared under its asset licence, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
  (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/>): credit kreyk29,
  no commercial use, and share anything made from them alike.
- The original's code is GPL v3. None of it is used here.
